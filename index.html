About the Educational Statistical Archive

Lotto AI Africa is an educational research initiative designed to archive historical numerical sequences from Pan-African matrix systems (such as Ghana NLA 5/90, Baba Ijebu 5/90, Daywa 5/39, and Super 6 6/28). The project offers analytical models—including row-stochastic Markov transition matrices, odd/even parity balance tracking, and positional vector distributions—to study probability theories across random sampling spaces.

Disclaimer: This platform does not sell lottery tickets, provide gambling services, or guarantee future numerical outcomes. All tools are intended strictly for statistical education and computational research.
