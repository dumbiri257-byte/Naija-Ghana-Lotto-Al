ℹ️ About Lotto AI Africa

Lotto AI Africa (accessible via https://lottoai.africa) is an independent, non-gambling mathematical analytics platform designed to archive, structure, and analyze historical draw results for major Pan-African regional lottery games, including Premier Lotto (Baba Ijebu), Golden Chance, and Ghana National Lottery Authority (NLA) games (5/90, 5/39 Daywa, and 6/28 Super 6 matrices).

Our web platform utilizes deterministic algorithms and multi-matrix Markov state transition probability models to allow researchers, statistical enthusiasts, and users to study historical number frequency, balance distributions, and draw sequences strictly for educational and informational research.
