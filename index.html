ℹ️ About Naija-Ghana Lotto AI Engine

Naija-Ghana Lotto AI Engine is an independent, non-gambling mathematical analytics suite designed to archive, structure, and analyze historical draw results for major regional 5/90, 5/39, and 6/28 lottery games including Baba Ijebu (Premier Lotto), Golden Chance, and Ghana NLA.

Our platform uses statistical algorithms (including multi-matrix Markov transition probability models) to help research historical frequency, number distribution balance, and draw sequences purely for educational, analytical, and informational purposes.
